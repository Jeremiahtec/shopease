Comprehensive Privacy Policy & Unified Terms of Service

These unified platform terms constitute a legally binding agreement between you (“User”, “Buyer”, or “Merchant”) and ShopEase Marketplace Inc. By accessing, cataloging, purchasing, or utilizing our digital marketplace, you confirm unequivocal adherence to all standards established herein.

01

Introduction & Overview

ShopEase operates a decentralized, managed commerce ecosystem designed to connect verified independent merchants with international consumers. The platform provides secure catalog hosting, buyer protection mechanisms, transaction intermediation, and automated escrow clearance.

By registering an account, integrating API credentials, or engaging in transactional checkouts, you agree to these Terms alongside our localized Data Protection annexes (consistent with GDPR, NDPR, and CCPA regulatory frameworks). If you disagree with any portion of these provisions, you must immediately terminate platform access and cease all merchant activities.

02

User Accounts & Multi-Vendor Roles

ShopEase provisions two primary interactive tiers across its architecture:

person Buyer Accounts

Permitted to curate shopping baskets, submit authenticated payment authorizations, initiate dispute resolutions, and maintain individualized delivery addresses. Buyers must provide accurate legal identification when processing purchases exceeding threshold limits.

store Merchant/Vendor Accounts

Subject to mandatory Corporate KYC/AML validation. Vendors retain legal title over cataloged inventory, must maintain accurate product telemetry, and abide by ShopEase standardized dispatch velocity and SLA frameworks.

  • You are solely responsible for preserving credential confidentiality, including multi-factor authentication tokens.
  • Impersonation, fraudulent corporate entity filing, or unauthorized access attempts will trigger immediate automated lockout and escalation to enforcement agencies.

04

Payments, Escrow & Paystack Processing

All payment settlements on ShopEase are facilitated through encrypted integrations with certified PCI-DSS Level 1 payment gateways, including Paystack Payments Limited and international banking partners. ShopEase does not capture, store, or serialize raw credit card numbers or security CVV pins on proprietary servers.

lock_clock

Automated Multi-Vendor Escrow Protocol

When a buyer successfully authorizes checkout, funds are quarantined in our automated Escrow Vault. Disbursements to the vendor's digital ledger occur exclusively when: (a) The delivery carrier issues an immutable "Delivered" webhook signature, and (b) The buyer's statutory 48-hour inspection window elapses without dispute submission.

Currency settlements occur automatically in standard regional denominations (USD, EUR, GBP, NGN, KES). Processing surcharges, currency conversion spreads, and vendor clearing commissions are deducted transparently prior to bank settlement batching.

05

Vendor Responsibilities & Storefront Rules

Merchants operating on the ShopEase ecosystem are bound to strict fulfillment service-level agreements (SLAs). Non-compliance induces dynamic rank degradation, temporary escrow withholding, or storefront de-indexing.

  1. Fulfillment Timelines: Physical items must be manifested and handed over to designated logistics partners within 72 hours of purchase confirmation.
  2. Catalog Integrity: Product imagery, physical specifications, and available inventory quantities must be strictly authentic. Replicas, unbranded trademark counterfeits, or deceptive photography are rigorously barred.
  3. Direct Solicitations: Merchants are strictly forbidden from attempting to route ShopEase buyers off-platform to execute external non-escrow transactions. Violations result in permanent payout confiscation.

07

Buyer Protection & Dispute Resolution

Every customer transaction processed via ShopEase Escrow is guarded by our Buyer Protection Scheme. Buyers may lodge a dispute ticket within 14 calendar days of marked delivery under the following conditions:

  • The delivered parcel did not arrive within the merchant's declared maximum SLA plus 5 business days grace.
  • The received parcel is damaged, non-functional, or fundamentally dissimilar from its catalog portrayal.
  • A vendor fails to provide an active, trackable waybill code within platform bounds.

Disputes are handled initially via internal merchant-buyer conciliation. Should consensus stall after 72 hours, the ShopEase Arbitral Panel evaluates photographic and telemetry evidence to decree final, irreversible escrow release or full buyer refund.
